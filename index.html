About the Office of Student Engagement

The Office of Student Engagement connects students with the people, programs, and organizations that make Northridge University feel like home. We partner with the Student Activities Board, Multicultural Student Council, Career Services, Campus Recreation, and Athletics to bring you a full calendar of events every semester.
