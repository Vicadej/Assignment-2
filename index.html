About the Office of Student Engagement

The Office of Student Engagement connects students with the people, programs, and organizations that make Northridge University feel like home. We partner with the Student Activities Board, Multicultural Student Council, Career Services, Campus Recreation, and Falcon Athletics to bring you a full calendar of events every semester.

Whether you're looking to make new friends, build your résumé, or just find something fun to do on a Friday night, our office is the one place to keep track of it all.

  • Free & low-cost events every week — from concerts to career fairs, most events cost nothing to attend.
  • One central events calendar — check a single page instead of a dozen different club social accounts.
  • Direct connections to campus organizations — including the Student Activities Board, Multicultural Student Council, Career Services, Campus Recreation, and Falcon Athletics.
  • Accessibility accommodations — sensory-friendly spaces and accessible seating available at every major event.
