Featured Event

Fall Welcome Fest

The Student Activities Board's biggest welcome-week celebration — live music, food trucks, and 40+ student organizations on the Quad.

About This Event

Join thousands of Falcons for the official kickoff to the fall semester. Fall Welcome Fest fills the Quad with music, food, and free giveaways as student organizations, campus departments, and local vendors set up booths to help you find your community for the year ahead.

Whether you're a first-year looking to make friends, a returning student catching up with old ones, or just here for the free food trucks, there's something for everyone. Rain plan: the event moves indoors to the Union Grand Hall, with an announcement posted on this page and emailed to all students by noon that day.

Good to Know

  • Free and open to all currently enrolled students — no RSVP required, just bring your student ID.
  • Food truck items range from free to $8; a few vendors are cash-only.
  • Accessible seating and a quiet sensory-friendly zone are available near the Library entrance to the Quad.
  • Lost and found is at the Student Activities Board info booth near the main stage.

Location

The Quad sits at the heart of campus, between the Library, the Student Union, and the Recreation Center — look for the string lights and main stage.
